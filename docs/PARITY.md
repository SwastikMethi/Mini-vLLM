## CPU fp32 vs MPS bf16 (M0, 2026-10-17)

| Prompt tokens | Greedy agreement |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | diverges at decode step 1 |
| 16, 17, 31, 32, 33, 65 | identical |

**15-token divergence — investigated, accepted.** At step 1 the CPU fp32 top-2 logits are 15.612 (id 1855) and
15.579 (id 990), a 0.033 margin. bf16 resolves 0.0625 near 16, so both round to 15.625 on MPS and the tie breaks
differently. This is dtype quantization, not a cache or position bug.

Policy: CPU fp32 is ground truth for parity tests. MPS tests compare logits within a bf16 tolerance and accept a greedy
divergence only when the CPU top-2 margin at that step is below the bf16 resolution at that magnitude.
